5e8f2b1phase/04release: autonomous agents

Autonomous — with humans in the loop.

Today our agents operate autonomously, while humans stay in the loop for critical decisions and oversight. We didn't replace engineers. We built a workflow where AI and people complement each other.

e4b7d2aphase/05refactor: the developer's role

My job changed. I write less — and review more.

Before agentic development, most of my day as a developer went into writing code by hand, ticket by ticket. Today, most of my time goes into reviewing the plan the AI is about to execute, and giving feedback on the result it produces. The judgment moved up a level: from writing the solution to shaping it, checking it, and deciding whether it's right.

--- a/developer.day # before agentic+++ b/developer.day # after agentic- implement: write the code by hand, ticket by ticket- debug: trace issues line by line+ review_plan: read & approve what the AI will execute # before it runs+ give_feedback: evaluate the result, correct, iterate # after it runs+ decide: architecture, edge cases, "is this right?" ship: still my name on the PR
